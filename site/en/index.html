Tour

One notepad
to run your day

01 · The notepad

A notepad that puts now first

One sheet of paper with Today and Ahead tabs. The day ribbon at the top writes what's on now in large handwriting, with the time left in a ring beside it. What's next and today's list follow below.

02 · Timetable

Your day, unfolded

Click the day ribbon and the day unfolds into a timetable right there. Drag a block and it follows the mouse, then settles on a quarter hour. Drag its top or bottom edge to change its length.

03 · Focus

Focus on one thing

Type ‘report 25 min focus’ and the notepad turns into a single card. When time is up, pick Done, 5 more min or Not now. It can run Pomodoro rounds too.

04 · Alarms

Alarms on a paper slip

Pick the days, the sound and the snooze. When it's time, a slip pops up with the cat shaking a bell. For the first 1.5 seconds it ignores the keyboard, so you can't dismiss it by accident while typing somewhere else.

05 · Ahead

Drag to reschedule

This week and next, day by day, with events and due dates in one place. Drag a to-do onto another day and its due date moves with it.

06 · Desk

Calendar and settings on the Desk

Calendar, history, settings and help live in the Desk window. Keep the notepad small on screen, and open the Desk when you need to look further ahead. The calendar also shows lunar dates and holiday names.

Features

Take out
only what you need

Sort your to-dos, see what comes back, pick a paper, and keep backups.

A cat taps along as you type

The tap-tap cat in a corner of the screen taps with its paws. Choose In this app or In all apps; it never knows which key.

Sorted, still one sheet

Sort to-dos into spaces like Work, Home and Study, and Today still shows them all. Write ‘#work’ and it goes into Work, or gather each space into a column of its own.

Night paper at night

When the system is in dark mode, every window turns the color of paper in a room with the lights off. Text contrast meets the web accessibility standard (WCAG AA) by day and by night.

Repeats on the days ahead

A repeating to-do like ‘Recycling every Monday’ shows on each day it comes back, on the calendar and the widgets. Only the next one is bold.

Fifteen papers

Pick one of eleven light papers or four dark ones, and the notepad, calendar, widgets and notes drawer all change to it.

Backed up once a day

Once a day, and before anything overwrites your list. Pick a backup to merge it in or to replace your list with it.

A paper cat nearby

It walks along the Dock (the taskbar on Windows) and brings your to-do when a reminder is due. Finish everything and it cheers you on.

Calendar widgets

A paper calendar
on your desktop

Pick from seven widgets, from Today to a wall calendar for the month, and keep up to four. Lunar dates and Korean public holidays show from the start. Add them in Desk → Events → Desktop widgets.

  • Click a day for its slipClick a day on a widget to see everything on it. Type there as you would in the notepad’s input line, and it goes straight in.
  • Four sizesEach widget can be Small, Regular, Large or Extra large. Text, cells and margins all scale together, so a large widget doesn’t leave cells empty or cut names short. Sizes that don’t fit your screen are dimmed.
  • Drag it by the earsDrag the cat’s ears at the top right to move a widget, or click them for a menu with Size and Keep on top. Widgets sit on the desktop, below other windows, at first.
  • Keep the notepad on the desktopThe pin at the top of the notepad offers Regular, Keep on top and On the desktop. On the desktop, the notepad stays behind other windows like a widget, and comes forward only for a moment when you open it with its shortcut.

Notes drawer

Notes live in a drawer
at the screen’s edge

Pull the drawer out from the edge of the screen in any app to read a note, jot one down, or edit it in place. Notes from the notepad’s old Notes tab are in the drawer’s Notes folder.

All features
  • Out from any appPress ⇧⌘D (Ctrl+Shift+D on Windows) or click the cat-paw handle at the edge. Clicking another app doesn’t close it.
  • Folders are bookmarksEach bookmark beside the drawer is a folder. Notes are grouped into Pinned, Today, Yesterday, Last week and then by month, and a link or phone number shows as a small label on the card.
  • Search by folderFound notes are grouped by folder. Filter them with the Unticked, Links and Pinned chips, or search only the folder you’re in.
  • Deleted notes for 30 daysDeleted notes stay for 30 days. Deleted notes in the folder name ▾ menu puts them back in their folder.
  • Looks the way you writeBold, headings, lists, checkboxes and code look that way as you type. Change them with the format (Aa) button on an open note or keys such as ⌘B.
  • Paper and backdropThe fifteen papers are the same as the notepad’s. The backdrop is Solid by default, with Frosted, Clear and None to choose from. Frosted on Windows hasn’t been seen on a real PC yet.

How it's built

Built twice,
kept identical

Swift (SwiftUI + AppKit) on the Mac, Electron + TypeScript on Windows, built separately. They share no code. Both have to pass the same tables of test cases, which keeps their behavior the same.

136 rowsof input-line cases both apps pass character for character
582 + 420Windows tests, plus about 420 Mac checks (Windows 1.30, Mac 1.45)
61% → 0.7%Mac idle CPU with the notepad open
WCAG AAfor the app's text colors, day and night (except deliberately faint hints). Anything below it fails the check
  • Case tables come firstHow ‘meeting tomorrow 3pm’ is read, down to the words on the chip, is one row. The Swift and TypeScript tests must both produce that exact string.
  • Screens are checked by rendering themOn the Mac, about 2,100 real screens are captured across 3 languages, day and night. On Windows, every window is redrawn at different sizes, scale factors and fonts to catch clipped text.
  • Where the 61% CPU came fromThe blinking ‘now’ line in the day ribbon was redrawing the whole notepad every frame. Drawing that line on its own brought idle CPU down to 0.7%. In the same release, typing latency on the Mac fell from 0.46 s to 0.01 s and launch from 2.7 s to 1.0 s. On Windows, version 1.24 cut memory after 10 minutes from 692 MB to 457 MB (measured in Electron on macOS).
  • Not done yetThe Windows version has only run in Electron on macOS, never on a real Windows PC. Neither app is code-signed.

Questions, answered

More privacy questions
Is it really free?

Yes. No payments and no locked features. The source code is on GitHub under the Apache License 2.0.

Do I need an account? Where are my notes kept?

No account and no server. Everything is saved as files in Jongi-nyang’s folder on your computer, or in a folder you choose. The privacy page has the details. Change it under Desk → Storage & backup.

Can I use it on a Mac and a Windows PC together?

Yes. Choose the same shared folder (iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and so on) as the storage location on both. Each device can also decide which spaces it shows.

Does it connect to Google Calendar?

No. I built it over five releases and it worked, but I took it out. I didn't want work plans mixing into my personal space, and the people testing it said the same. The full reasoning is in the decision log (Korean).

Why is it blocked the first time I open it?

The builds aren't code-signed. On the Mac, right-click the app in Applications and choose Open; if it’s still blocked, click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway in SmartScreen.

Which languages and systems?

Korean, English and Dutch. It runs on macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon, and on Windows 10/11 x64. The Windows version has only been checked in Electron on macOS, not yet on a real Windows PC.

Check what you downloaded

Each release lists the SHA-256 value of every file, in the checksums file (SHA256SUMS) and in the release notes. If your file gives the same value, it’s the one we uploaded. If it differs, don’t open it; delete it.

  • Mac Terminal: shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/JongiNyang-1.49.1.dmg
  • Windows PowerShell: Get-FileHash "$HOME\Downloads\JongiNyang-1.34.1-x64.exe" -Algorithm SHA256
